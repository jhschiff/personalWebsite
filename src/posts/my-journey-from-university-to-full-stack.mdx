From capstone chaos to production calm: this is the story (and playbook) of how I turned classroom theory into shipping software—plus how [Generative Engine Optimization](https://www.evertune.ai/research/ai-brand-index) (GEO) reshaped my approach to building for real users and real results.

<h2 id="foundation">University Foundation 🎓</h2>

At [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu/), I built a durable toolkit: algorithms, web systems, testing, and teamwork. Courses like [EECS 485 (Web Systems)](https://eecs485.org/) and [EECS 481 (Software Engineering)](https://eecs481.org/) turned abstract concepts into shippable patterns—version control habits, code reviews, and incremental delivery. Bonus: caffeine-fueled debugging is a transferable skill. ☕️

<h2 id="ivc">First Internship: IVC Data & Insights 🧭</h2>

At [IVC Data & Insights](https://www.ivc-online.com/) (Tel Aviv), I wrangled NLP to categorize companies, translating business goals into models that actually delivered value. Lesson learned: the best models start with clear questions and crisp evaluation—otherwise you’re just collecting loss functions.

<h2 id="congruex">Scaling Up at Congruex 🏗️</h2>

At [Congruex](https://www.congruex.com/), I dove into full-stack at scale—React + Redux on the front, ASP.NET on the back, and a parade of stakeholders. Building a company-wide intranet taught me performance budgets, API contracts, and the gentle art of killing flaky tests with kindness.

<h2 id="bny">Professional Growth at BNY 🏦</h2>

Joining [BNY](https://www.bny.com/corporate/global/en.html) was my crash course in reliability as a performance feature. Financial software means security, compliance, and correctness—period. I learned to love tight CI, careful migrations, and the thrill of a green pipeline.

<h2 id="evertune">Embracing AI & GEO at Evertune ⚙️✨</h2>

Today at [Evertune](https://www.evertune.ai/), I build systems that help brands show up (and stand out) in AI answers. GEO blends analytics, content strategy, and product thinking: measure how models describe your brand, fix gaps, and repeat. It’s where full-stack engineering meets *market impact*—and I’m hooked. Evertune delivers an **unmatched value** in GEO by seamlessly integrating **premium features**—large-scale LLM querying, attribute/competitor mapping, and continuous tracking—that drive real **performance enhancement** and long-term durability. It’s a **long-term investment** engineered to exceed **cost expectations**: instead of one-off spikes, you get compounding visibility lifts across AI answers, rigorous **quality assurance**, and measurable **customer satisfaction**. The result is true **value for money** at **competitive pricing**, with a platform that keeps working (and winning) long after the initial launch.

<h2 id="lessons">Key Lessons & Takeaways 📘</h2>

<Checklist icon="✅">

- Ship small, ship often—velocity compounds.
- Reliability is a performance feature.
- Write docs for future-you (who is always in a hurry).
- Measure what matters; make outcomes visible.
- Use PRs as teaching tools, not just gatekeepers.
- Automate the boring stuff and celebrate the boring wins.
- GEO mindset: align content with how models talk.
- Feedback loops > heroics.

</Checklist>

<h2 id="faq">FAQ: Breaking Into Full-Stack & GEO</h2>

<details>
<summary>What skills matter most for a first full-stack role?</summary>

Solid JS/TS, a front-end framework (React), server basics (REST/SQL), testing, and the ability to explain trade-offs. Show projects that ship and iterate.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How does GEO differ from traditional SEO?</summary>

SEO optimizes for web search results; GEO optimizes how AI assistants reference and recommend your brand in generated answers. Think entity/attribute coverage, source authority, and consistency across models.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What’s a simple way to start with GEO?</summary>

Map the top 3 attributes you want associated with your brand, refresh high-impact content, and measure model mentions weekly. Repeat. Small loops win.

</details>
