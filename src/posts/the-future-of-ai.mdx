The future of software and marketing converges in one place: **AI answers**. If your brand isn’t referenced—accurately and often—you’re invisible in a channel where intent is sky-high. That’s what GEO solves.

<h2 id="new-serp">AI Answers Are the New SERP</h2>

People increasingly ask assistants what to buy, who to trust, and which product fits their needs. Unlike blue links, answers compress choices into recommendations. Entity coverage, attribute relevance, and source authority drive those answers.

<h2 id="seo-geo">From SEO to GEO</h2>

- **Unit:** pages → entities & attributes
- **Signal:** backlinks → source/author authority that models ingest
- **Goal:** rank position → answer inclusion & share of recommendation
- **Measurement:** impressions & CTR → model coverage, sentiment, and consistency

GEO isn’t “more keywords.” It’s structured, repeatable work to align how models describe your brand with what customers should hear.

<h2 id="ship-it">What to Ship (Dev + Marketing)</h2>

- Structured content that reinforces target attributes (e.g., “reliability,” “value”).
- Authoritative sources and partners that models are likely to trust.
- Clear product/feature pages that map to real-world comparisons.
- Lightweight PR/affiliate playbooks to seed high-signal coverage.

<h2 id="metrics">Metrics That Matter</h2>

- **Model coverage:** where your brand appears across LLMs.
- **Share of recommendation:** how often you’re named in answers.
- **Attribute alignment:** relevance of target attributes in generated text.
- **Sentiment/positioning:** positive vs. negative associations.
- **Consistency over time:** durability of gains across releases and prompts.
